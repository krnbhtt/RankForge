# Phase 6 exit test: can a newcomer explain both failures?

**Criterion (specification 10, Phase 6):** after one run, a person who has never seen the product can explain both why the page is not ranking and why it is not converting, without help.

This needs real people, so it has not been passed yet. This kit makes it a 15-minute session per person. Run it with 5 people; the criterion is met if at least 4 of 5 pass.

## Who to ask

People who run, or have worked on, an online shop but have not seen RankForge. Not teammates. Do not explain the product first.

## Setup (once)

1. Open the studio on a **prepared example** (Start screen, "Prepared examples"), so the run does not depend on credits. Pick one whose score went up.
2. Have the studio open on the Summary tab, in a normal browser window (about 1400 px wide).
3. Start a screen or audio recording only if the participant agrees.

## Script (read it as written)

> "This tool looked at a product page and a set of competitor pages. I will not explain it. Please look at the screen for up to three minutes, thinking aloud. Then I will ask you three questions. I cannot answer questions about the screen until the end."

After three minutes (or when they say they are done) ask:

1. "In your own words, why is this page not ranking higher in search?"
2. "Why do you think visitors are not buying?"
3. "What did the tool change on the page, and did the score go up?"

Then ask: "What was confusing?" and "What would you have wanted it to say?"

## Scoring (per person)

| Question | Pass when the person says |
|---|---|
| 1. Ranking | at least two of the reasons shown in the first section of the Summary tab, in their own words |
| 2. Conversion | at least two of the reasons shown in the second section, in their own words |
| 3. Change | that sections were added or fixed on the right-hand page, and the direction of the score |

A person **passes** if they answer 1 and 2 correctly without being prompted. Question 3 is recorded but does not decide the result.

Write down the exact words they used, the time they took, and every moment they hesitated or scrolled the wrong way.

## What to change if people fail

- If they cannot find the two explanations: make the Summary tab larger or move it above the preview.
- If they say the right-hand page looks the same: the added sections are below the fold; consider scrolling the optimised preview to the first tag when the audit completes.
- If they do not understand a score: add one plain sentence under it saying what is measured.
